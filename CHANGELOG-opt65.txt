SUNNY opt65 - radar from one seamless North American composite, honest coverage

- radar-source.js (new): radar now comes from Environment and Climate Change Canada
  MSC GeoMet, layer RADAR_1KM_RRAI (precipitation rate, 1 km, every 6 minutes, last 3 hours).
  It is one composite of Canadian radars and US NEXRAD, so there is one colour scale and no
  seam at the border. RainViewer is no longer used (its smoothed Canadian data drew puffy
  circular blobs).
- Areas no radar reaches are shown with GeoMet's coverage layer (RADAR_COVERAGE_RRAI.INV,
  hatch style) at 45% opacity while Radar is selected, so a range edge never looks clear.
- Play composite: one WMS GetMap per frame for the whole view instead of up to 64 tiles.
- Fix (regression from opt63): the radar Play renderer had the cloud-only key switched on,
  which erased coloured radar during Play. Only the clouds renderer uses it now.
- Attribution: "Radar: ECCC MSC GeoMet (incl. NOAA NEXRAD), OGL-Canada"
  (Open Government Licence - Canada).
- Future deploys: run node tools/write-version.js (unchanged rule).
