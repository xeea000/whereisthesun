SUNNY opt38 — scrub throttle, play prefetch, gesture pause, mobile WMS, idle warm
================================================================================
Five smoothness / responsiveness fixes. Keeps opt35–37 pace, blur-fade, unstick, locate.

1) Scrub rAF throttle
   - Slider input stores desired index; one requestAnimationFrame applies per frame.
   - endScrub / change / pointerup flush pending frame immediately (final index).
   - opt28 scrub-was-playing resume preserved.

2) Play prefetch overlap
   - prefetchPlayIndices at tick start and as soon as frame reveal/load begins
     (not only when shown=true). Also after schedule.
   - While current frame loads, still warms n=1..PLAY_PREFETCH along playFrameStep.

3) Pan/zoom: pause heavy work
   - mapGesturing true on movestart/zoomstart; false on moveend/zoomend.
   - While gesturing: skip maybeIdlePrefetchRing; defer new sunny weather batches;
     MOVE_DEBOUNCE moveend remains the resume point. Play map-lock (opt23/35) intact.

4) Faster mobile Play WMS
   - playImageSize: narrow (<700) ~75% of desktop size; keep opt37 ≥2x 0.72 shrink.
   - Min-edge clamp ~512 when base allows (else ≥64).

5) Idle GOES warm-cache
   - After ~1.7s idle (post moveend / tab visible), quiet-prefetch ~3 nearby
     cloudTimes WMS into playImgCache via loadImageSrc / gibsWmsFrameMeta.
   - Cancel on scrub / play / hide tab / gesture.
