Prebuilt forest loss+driver index tiles for SUNNY.
Source: Global Forest Watch tiles (CC BY 4.0). Rebuilt offline; phone gets only these.
Encoding: WebP lossless LA — L packs loss year (1-24 = 2001-2024) in low 5 bits and
driver class (0-5) in high 3 bits; A=255 where loss. IFL under ifl/ as mask.
Missing file inside coverage = no loss / no IFL. Outside coverage = client may fall back.
