SUNNY opt18 — radar XOR clouds (fix dual-layer lag)
=====================================================
Root cause of Radar lag with clouds up: RainViewer tiles + multi
GIBS/MODIS/VIIRS/IEM rasters (+ Play WMS) competed for bandwidth/GPU.

Mutex:
- Radar ON: mute clouds (opacity 0, hide rasters), stop Play/scrub
  tile work, skip GIBS/hires refresh; remember prior opacity.
- Radar OFF: restore saved cloud opacity and resume cloud tiles.
- Raising Clouds slider > 0 while Radar on → turns Radar OFF.
- Play while Radar on → auto-turns Radar OFF (Play needs clouds).
- Persist: if Radar saved on, boot with clouds hidden (Radar wins).

Cache: ?v=opt18; SW CACHE=sunny-v-opt18
Preserved: opt17 deferred MapLibre (no preload), opt16 RainViewer.
